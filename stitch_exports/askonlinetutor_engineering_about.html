Our Story

What began as a focused utility—Beam Master—designed to help structural engineering students visualize shear and moment diagrams, quickly evolved. Recognizing a broader need for tools that didn't compromise on professional standard while remaining pedagogically sound, we expanded.

Today, Ask Online Tutor: Engineering encompasses a comprehensive suite of analysis tools, section property calculators, and dynamic physics simulations, serving a global community of learners and practitioners.
